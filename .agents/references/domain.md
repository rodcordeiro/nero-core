# Domain

## Purpose

Nero is a generic knowledge base motor for agents: MCP server, `$nero` skill, playbooks, guidelines and an empty Knowledge Repo scaffold.

## What belongs here

- Generic schema and tooling for knowledge registration, search, graph links, validation and admin workflows.
- Skill instructions and playbooks that apply across projects/domains.
- Guidelines for broad domains such as API, front, mobile, powershell and MCP.
- Documentation for how users can create external Domain Skills.

## What does not belong here

- Real product corpus, company-specific notes or personal Knowledge Repo content.
- Product, organization or library-specific Domain Skill contents.
- Secrets, tokens, cookies, Authorization values, sensitive URLs, sensitive payloads or personal data.

Do not invent business rules. Register uncertain behavior as a hypothesis only when durable knowledge is actually needed; otherwise leave it out.
